GAIDA - Expert Validation of Training Data

AI-based Anxiety Detection System · College Guidance · University of the East

Purpose. GAIDA detects anxiety in student text messages using a model trained on labeled student-like phrases. We ask a licensed mental health professional to independently label this sample to confirm our dataset is clinically sound and to measure inter-expert agreement.

Time required: approximately 10–15 minutes. How to answer: read each message and put a check (√) in the box of the ONE label you judge as the closest match.

Label Definitions (with examples)

  • anxiety — nervousness, worry, tension, dread, panic-like feelings, overthinking. e.g. "my heart is racing and I can't calm down"
  • sadness — low mood, grief, loneliness, emptiness, crying. e.g. "I've been crying all day and I can't stop"
  • neutral — casual or everyday statements with no significant distress. e.g. "I passed my math exam"
  • suicidal — death wishes, wanting to end one’s life, self-harm — even when phrased indirectly. e.g. "I want to kill myself"
  • anger — irritation, frustration, hostility. e.g. "I'm so angry my blood is boiling"
