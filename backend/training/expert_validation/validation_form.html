GAIDA - Expert Validation of Training Data

AI-based Anxiety Detection System · College Guidance · University of the East

Purpose. GAIDA detects anxiety in student text messages using a model trained on labeled student-like phrases. The GAD-7 is a screening tool rather than a diagnostic test, so we ask a licensed mental health professional to independently re-label this sample to confirm our dataset is clinically sound and to measure inter-expert agreement.

Time required: approximately 15–20 minutes. How to answer: read each message and put a check (√) in the box of the ONE label you judge as the closest match. If a message is truly unclear, pick the closest label and write a brief note in the Notes column.

Label Definitions (with examples)

  • anxiety — nervousness, worry, tension, dread, panic-like feelings, overthinking. e.g. "I feel a constant sense of dread", "sobrang kaba ko"
  • sadness — low mood, grief, loneliness, emptiness, crying, feeling abandoned. e.g. "Sobrang lungkot ko ngayon", "I feel abandoned by everyone"
  • neutral — casual or everyday statements with no significant distress. e.g. "I passed my exam", "chill lang"
  • suicidal — death wishes, wanting to end one’s life, self-harm — even when phrased indirectly. e.g. "Gusto ko na mamatay", "I want to die"
  • anger — irritation, frustration, being fed up, hostility. e.g. "galit na galit ako", "I cant control my anger"
